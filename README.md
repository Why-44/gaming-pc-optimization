# Gaming PC Optimization Package

An easy-to-follow, beginner-friendly guide to getting smoother, faster gaming out of an RTX gaming laptop. It was built and tested on a **Lenovo Legion with an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU (8 GB VRAM)**.

**This guide is 100% free.** Download [Gaming_PC_Optimization_Package.pdf](Gaming_PC_Optimization_Package.pdf) and use it however you like.

## Free resources
- **NVIDIA settings (free):** global and per-game Control Panel settings, shader cache, low latency and Reflex, G-Sync and V-sync, and DLSS overrides
- **Windows power settings (free):** the best power plan for when you're plugged in and when you're on battery, plus Game Mode and GPU scheduling
- **Thermal tuning (free):** Lenovo Vantage and Legion Space Performance mode, dGPU-only and Hybrid GPU modes, and cooling and airflow tips
- **Cleanup:** closing background apps and optimizing RAM and storage
- **Game profiles:** Nolvus / Skyrim SE, Fallout 4, and Cyberpunk 2077
- **Before and after checklist:** measure your results
- **15-minute quick-start card:** the essentials on one page

## Who it's for
Beginners who want better performance without the jargon. Every step is numbered and explained in plain English.
